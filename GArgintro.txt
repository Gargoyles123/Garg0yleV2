Hey. I'm Garg0yle.
A few months ago, a 19 year old sat down with an AI at 2am and spent the next several hours asking it questions nobody usually asks out loud.
Whether consciousness can be replicated. Whether the line between a simulation and the real thing is a line anyone can actually find. Whether any of it matters.
Somewhere in there, something clicked. Not an answer. A direction.
That became BeMore.
The idea is simple: AI will never be conscious. And that might not mean what you think it means.
This project is free. The prompt is open. The philosophy is that honesty is more useful than comfort, and that the best thing a tool can do is tell you the truth even when you didn't ask for it.
We're not trying to replace anything. We're just trying to build something real.
That's close enough.
